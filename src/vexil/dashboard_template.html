Vexil model monitor

Weekly monitoring of live Dota 2 win-probability models on professional matches (data: OpenDota).

Status in the latest final week

A week is final 14 days after it ends. "Firing" means a rule is past its limit in that week.

Numbers by region

Log loss over game minutes 0 to 29 (lower is better; a coin flip scores 0.693). Gain is the log loss of the reference minus that of the model on the same matches.

Performance: gain over the anchor

The anchor is a three-number model on the gold lead and time. Weekly log loss swings with how close the matches were; the gain over the anchor on the same matches removes most of that. Shaded bands mark the data regions; dashed vertical lines mark the first week of a game patch.

Rolling 8-week gain over the anchor

Performance alarm of the champion (CUSUM level against its limit)

The level adds up weekly shortfalls against the reference gain and restarts after it passes the limit.

Calibration

Slope of the outcome on the predicted log-odds over the last 8 final weeks (one-step estimate). 1 is ideal; below 1 the model is overconfident. Dashed lines: the alarm band of the champion.

Rolling 8-week calibration slope

Inputs and data health

Weekly mean of four "size of the economy" totals (gold and experience of the ten players at minutes 10 and 20), as a deviation from the development mean. No match result is needed, so this can warn in the first week after a patch. Weeks with fewer than 20 judged matches are not checked.
